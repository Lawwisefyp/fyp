LAWWISE

Unified Practice Management & RAG-Driven Legal Education Ecosystem

Introduction

LawWise is an innovative legal technology platform designed to digitalize and unify legal practice management and legal education within Pakistan. Integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with professional document templating, the platform delivers multi-tenant web portals tailored for lawyers, clients, and students. By streamlining case progress tracking and providing AI-guided workflows, LawWise bridges the gap between client advocacy and academic legal preparedness.

Problem Statement

The Pakistani legal system faces severe bottlenecks, marked by archaic manual tracking, a lack of digitized public court directories, and high accessibility barriers to verified legal literature. Concurrently, law students struggle with fragmented exam preparation tools and a lack of interactive, localized training materials. Existing systems are segmented and vulnerable to internet outages, highlighting the urgent need for a unified, offline-resilient, and intelligent legal ecosystem.

Main Objectives

⚖️

AI Legal Drafting

Automated synthesis of court pleadings and legal agreements using GPT-4o with deterministic placeholders extraction.

⚡

Triple-Fail-Safe AI

Robust offline configuration automatically failing over from Gemini Cloud, to Groq, and local Ollama model networks.

🔍

Retrieval-Augmented RAG

Vector database lookup in Chroma DB of core statutes (Constitution and PPC) to secure verified, hallucination-free advisor chat outputs.

👥

Smart Lawyer Match

Rated web directory matching citizens with vetted advocates matching specialized case criteria, local bar credentials, and reviews.

📋

Intake Classification

Intelligent case categorization and risk-priority assessment based on NLP classification of unstructured client descriptions.

🎓

LexAcademy Suite

Syllabus exam guides, mock quiz modules (LAT), study task streak calendars, and automated student credit GPA calculations.

💬

Advisory Decision Logic

Reasoning chatbots enforcing civil vs. criminal intent testing, Qanun-e-Shahadat evidence standards, and quashing analysis.

📂

Collaborative Library

Note-sharing library featuring custom directory folders, download trackers, and instant triggers to share resources via WhatsApp/Email.

📅

Practice Management

Interactive case stage logs, litigation hearing calendars, client connection invitations, and digital consultation bookings.
